# Product Thinking Resources

## Knowledge

- [IDG: Product Thinking pathway overview](https://www.idg.gov.sg/product-thinking/)
  The hub page: learning objectives (4Cs, outputs vs outcomes, Five Whys) and links to all 7 guides. Use for: the shape of the whole pathway. Local text copy: `sources/idg/00-overview.txt`.
- [IDG Guide 1: Understanding the Problem](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking1/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DqGP6BvyRdk)
  Problems before solutions; the 3 principles; successful products start narrow. Use for: lesson 1.
- [IDG Guide 2: Start With The Whys](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking2/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdzcM6ax5YY)
  Five Whys; choosing a cause within your control. Use for: root cause analysis.
- [IDG Guide 3: Craft a Clear Problem Statement](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking3/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBSAouM7iaE)
  4Cs framework (Clarity, Consequence, Cause, Confirmation), with a worked example on repeated form filling. Use for: problem statements.
- [IDG Guide 4: Metrics](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking4/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rtb_tlSzTJ0)
  SMART, leading and lagging indicators, Value-Cost Ratio. Use for: measuring outcomes.
- [IDG Guide 5: Assumptions and Risks](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking5/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_iZNhzdLd7A)
  Market, technical and team risk; 4 stages of de-risking; health appointment booking case study. Use for: de-risking.
- [IDG Guide 6: A Good Customer Experience](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking6/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wMe7WgWzIe8)
  Why CX matters for mandatory public services; ParkingSG and RedeemSG; the 11-star framework. Use for: CX.
- [IDG Guide 7: Key Takeaways](https://www.idg.gov.sg/guides-and-resources/productthinking7/) · [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sI5veUTIzkk)
  The toolkit and the 3 mindset shifts. Use for: review and spaced retrieval.

- [IDG YouTube playlist: Product Thinking 101](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPCy3_08hWx_zOwXQM2jXKuwWYMil6HAd)
  The 7 guide videos (about 26 minutes in total). Transcripts are in `sources/idg/transcripts/`. They hold the detail the diagrams show, such as the 4Cs questions, the Five Whys examples and the risk types. Use for: exact IDG wording in every lesson.
