# Mission: Product Thinking (IDG)

## Why
Product thinking is part of a course I'm taking, the Agentic coding course. I need to understand and apply the Institute of Digital Government's product thinking pathway, so that I decide what is worth building, and why, before building anything.

## Success looks like
- Explain IDG's three principles and three mindset shifts in my own words.
- Take a vague "let's build X" idea and turn it into a sharp, data-backed problem statement using the 4Cs.
- Run a Five Whys on a real problem and pick the right level to fix.
- Choose an outcome metric, and say which indicators lead to it and which lag behind.
- Name the riskiest assumption in an idea and the cheapest way to test it.
- Complete the course deliverable. _Still to confirm: what it is. The folder is called `product-thinking-game`, which suggests building a game that teaches this material._

## Constraints
- Brand new to product work.
- Lessons of 5–10 minutes each.
- Ground lessons in IDG's own material (https://www.idg.gov.sg/product-thinking/).

## Out of scope
- General product management career skills (roadmaps, stakeholder politics, pricing).
- Frameworks IDG doesn't use, unless they're needed to understand IDG's.
