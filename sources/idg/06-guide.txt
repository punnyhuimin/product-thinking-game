## Good customer experience
Good customer experience isn't a nice-to-have, it's necessary. This matters especially for essential public services, where people may not have alternatives.
[IMG: Design principle: produc thinks for the user, solve specific frustrations, make smart trade offs, especially when its mandatory]
## Examples in Government
Strong government digital products often start by addressing a specific, real problem for users.
[IMG: use cases: ParkingSG, RedeemSG]
## 11-star framework
The 11-star framework is a way to stretch our thinking by imagining an exceptionally good customer experience, then working backwards to what is realistically worth building.
