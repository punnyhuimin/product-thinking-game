## Learning Objectives

### Objective 1
Apply the 4Cs Framework to write a clear, data-backed problem statement before any build begins.
### Objective 2
Distinguish between outputs and outcomes — and define success by the change achieved, not the features delivered.
### Objective 3
Use root cause analysis techniques like the Five Whys to move from surface symptoms to the real underlying problem.
## Product Thinking: 1: Understanding the Problem

Learn why great digital solutions start with problems, not technologies. This module introduces the product thinking mindset and its three core principles: problems before solutions, outcomes over outputs, and Policy-Ops-Tech integration.
