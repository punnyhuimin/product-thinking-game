"Product thinking is a discipline of asking better questions before reaching for solutions." IDG Guide 7: Key Takeaways

Three principles Guide 1

1

Focus on the problem, not the solution

Ask: Who are our users? What are they trying to do? What is getting in their way?

2

Chase outcomes, not outputs

Success is "the change achieved, not the features delivered."

3

Integrate policy, ops, and tech

"No initiative succeeds when these three work in silos."
